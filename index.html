Prototype boundaries

Version 0.2 uses synthetic in-app sample data only. No production connections, authentication, persistence, export workflow, causal analysis, or formal statistical validation. Contacts may include repeat participation. Survey responses are not necessarily representative; before/after score differences are descriptive, not proof of program impact. A real system would require stakeholder-defined measures, privacy/consent review, documented data definitions, accessibility testing, and human validation against source records.
