Prototype boundaries

Version 0.1 uses only synthetic in-app sample data. It has no production connections, authentication design, persistence requirement, export workflow, causal analysis, or formal statistical validation. Contacts may include repeat participation. Scores and behavior reports are descriptive, not proof of program impact. Review all figures before real-world use.
